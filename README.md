### 👋 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer with 5+ years of experience at **ColoredCow** and **Authenticode**, shipping production software across government, non-profit, e-commerce, and AI/EdTech platforms. I care about owning things end to end — architecture, delivery, and talking directly to the people who use what I build.

Most recently, as **Lead Engineer** at **Madhi Foundation**, I architected and shipped **VallamAI**, an AI-powered EdTech platform deployed across **37,000+ Tamil Nadu government schools**, built on a provider-agnostic LLM core spanning OpenAI and Anthropic Claude, a multi-agent lesson-plan generator, and a production RAG pipeline with a custom MCP server.

- 🔭 Currently working on **AI/LLM engineering** — LangChain, RAG, MCP, multi-agent systems, and LLM observability/evaluation.
- 🏛️ Tech Lead on the Government of India's **Project Monitoring Group (PMG) Portal**, integrating two national platforms and migrating 3,000+ users with zero downtime.
- 🌱 Tech Lead for 2.5+ years on **SankalpTaru**, a tree-plantation donation platform, owning storefront, backend, admin dashboard, and mobile field app.
- 🌍 Built **MERN stack** community platforms for **Shashwat Foundation** (AI Student Community, AI Facilitator Community).
- ✅ Practice **Test-Driven Development** and believe in reviewing code as carefully as I write it — 672 pull requests reviewed for teammates.
